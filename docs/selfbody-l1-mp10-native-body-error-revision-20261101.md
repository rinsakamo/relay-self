## Why MP10

MP9 physically routed a frozen body-motion coefficient by exact Paper material. This directional prediction improvement was too small to pass preregistered specificity, and the selected predictor was equivalent to a one-bit cheap material rule. MP10 must **not** simply tune a better coefficient on the same World and call it transfer. Its first objective is to wire actual typed next-native-epoch SelfBody evidence to a bounded, non-authoritative L1 error/correction candidate, keeping original L0/S49 closed.

Trigger at mean absolute error ≥ `0.002m` (fixed before new World), with positive finite `Σx²` and bounded admissible slope in [0,2]. If no meaningful evidence, KEEP or ABSTAIN. Else fit `Σxy / Σx²` as a **proposed next-epoch slope**. Freeze SHA256 canonical ticket containing original native Body source ref, Paper prefix SHA, old model ticket, session/World/subject and error metrics; emit one `MP10_L1_REVISION_NEXT_EPOCH` journal record after original WAIT observations.

No runtime L0 decision, original S49 Action count, S10/S11 Habit, L10 training, M0/P1 owner or persistent memory changes.
6. The candidate's MAE on its **fitting seven pairs is in-sample**. It may be numerically better merely because it is the same-data least squares fit; do not call this transfer, generalization, Goal success or unique intelligence. Same-information one-line OLS repair is exactly equivalent. The next **distinct heldout World** with proposal frozen before any target samples is mandatory before any error-correction improvement claim.
