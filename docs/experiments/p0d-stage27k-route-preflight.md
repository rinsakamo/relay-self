## Operator fixture preflight

`experiments/stage27k_route_preflight.py` is a pure offline checker, invoked with a JSON fixture path. It checks matching supplied World UUIDs, snapshot completeness, finite explicit dimensions and coordinates, exact continuous swept body box, obstacles, headroom and full-footprint support; absent cells or shapes are UNKNOWN. Supports only level axis-aligned paths up to 5m; steps, even climbable ones, are rejected and diagonal routes UNKNOWN. Known partial boxes outside the swept body are accepted. Collision boxes are explicit world-coordinate `[minX,minY,minZ,maxX,maxY,maxZ]`, keyed by `x,y,z`; `[]` means known empty and missing/null means unknown. Each intersecting cell must be supplied, including below feet. Input `start`, `target`, `width`, `height`, `expected_world_uuid`, `snapshot_world_uuid`, `snapshot_complete`, `cells` are required. Snapshot/UUID provenance and actual pose/dimensions must be independently verified by the operator: this checker does **not** read or authenticate a World, detect unloaded chunks itself, derive collision shapes, capture entities, or establish snapshot currentness. Missing world/fixture returns UNKNOWN; do not fabricate a complete fixture or trust a self-declared UUID. It is a geometry contract, not yet a complete automated World preflight. No Self Present/Memory injection, motor routing or avoidance behavior exists.

Offline adversarial tests cover flat path, contact after 0.2m, intermediate/side/ceiling/step/partial obstacles, partial clear, support missing, unknown/unloaded cell, UUID mismatch, missing input and numeric boundaries. Run `PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_stage27k_route_preflight.py`. The conservative full-footprint support rule may reject otherwise walkable edges, multi-box floor unions, steps and dynamic-world scenarios; CLEAR assumes supplied complete authoritative collision boxes and excludes no dynamic entities unless the snapshot provider explicitly includes them. UNKNOWN is not clearance.
