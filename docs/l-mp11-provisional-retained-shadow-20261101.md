2026-11-01. Stacked on MP11 G3 Draft #666; child of #431 / #653 / #537. This increment does not merge main, mutate another lane, issue a native Action, or access an LLM.

## Narrow result

adapters/mineflayer/self_body_mp11_retained_shadow.py implements a retention → next epoch → frozen prediction → later outcome-evaluation boundary using existing MP11 Stage0 typed objects:

1. Copy closed L2 proposals, owner-supplied receipts and results into an immutable ProvisionalModel; bind identities, Action IDs, Paper/native SHA shapes, contiguous 8 WAIT ticks, and source status. Reject pending, foreign, malformed, replayed or non-monotonic claims.
2. Retain the CHEAP exact-condition mean of earlier WAIT displacement. Keep the L2 model-family as a LABEL ONLY, never a learned structural hypothesis.
3. For an independent future World/session and the same subject, freeze an exact-condition prediction plus zero-motion and global-mean CHEAP comparators BEFORE future result access. For unknown conditions return ABSTAIN_ESCALATE_L2, a recommendation with no execution.
4. Score a future owner-supplied typed receipt using the immutable forecast and original identity/condition checks. Never fit heldout data back into training. Record negative cheap wins without pretending an L1 learning advantage.

## Strict limits

- All inputs carry UNVERIFIED_MP11_OWNER_FIXTURES. A valid 64-character SHA string is NOT independent Paper/native source authentication. The scorer does not parse raw original source. Attempts to relabel fixtures as qualified are rejected.
- Future proposal SHA and heldout receipt are owner-supplied claims, not independently linked to a live Gemma call. Scoring is a pure function; duplicate heldout scoring and physical admission require a different verified owner-level mechanism.
- Stage0's four distinct experiment labels do not prove real conditional adaptation, and physical G3's three same-GRASS MOVE_BACKWARD trials do not establish different conditions. Existing negative results are unchanged.
- ABSTAIN_ESCALATE_L2 neither calls a provider nor schedules a trial. No changes to L0, L1 Habit, Memory, Action issuer, durable training or safety boundaries.
- New physical Minecraft Worlds: 0. Real Gemma calls: 0. GOAL_UNDETERMINED / QUALIFICATION_NO_GO / SCIENCE_NOT_RUN.

## Why this L increment

The goal is developmental learning over epochs, not one-shot superiority over a cheap fixed L0. A cheap baseline may rationally win in familiar simple situations. Future work should measure genuine hypothesis revision, retained applicability, unseen-context abstention, shift-driven re-escalation and long-run cognitive costs. A single positive synthetic heldout score does not establish structural learning, transfer or intelligence.

Next: bind the real Gemma L2 OPEN decision to prior original source evidence and an owner-controlled physically varied next trial, establish independently validated source readback and frozen heldout comparisons. No automatic L0 promotion.
