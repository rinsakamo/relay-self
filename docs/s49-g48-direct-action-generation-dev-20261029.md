### Source limitations and next engineering decision

G4.8 only improves **observability**; direct original applied effect ACK proves Node attempted/changed its local control, while a same-effect post-control challenge with two interval Paper gait stability and original T2-applied gait coincidence is a bounded source-matched **candidate**, not packet-accepted gait for every Minecraft physics tick. Almost all remaining UNKNOWN ticks occur **outside original timed in-action RUN/G44 or ordinary JOG/WALK ACK windows**; those source intervals must not be retroactively labeled from a later Action, motor intent, nearby target, a missing sample or previous stable gait. Baseline and controlled Worlds have independent spawn and scheduling; WorldTick counts cannot be interpreted as a comparative speed improvement.

Next: preserve a single original S49/T2 owner, and reduce uncertainty only through *source-rich passive temporal brackets* (original Action lifecycle and Paper tick identity), if such evidence can be obtained without introducing synchronous tracing, extra sample-reader loops, longer artificial Action windows, or independent qualification claims. No automatically authorized P1/M0 regime change.
